The story

I didn't want another chatbot.
I wanted a team.

So I organized my bots like a company. Ask one bot for everything and you get one generalist. Give each bot a job, a boss and a lane, and they start to work like staff.

01 · DELEGATE

A Chief of Staff on top

One point of contact. You say what you need; the Chief of Staff routes it to the right direct report and follows up.

02 · SPECIALIZE

Executives own a lane

Entrepreneur, Health Coach, CAO, Concierge, Sports Agent, CTO, CFO, Legal Advisor. Each one knows its area and stays in it.

03 · SCALE

Every lane runs a team

Reports have their own specialists: a Chef under Health, a Travel Agent under the CAO, a Stockbroker under the Entrepreneur.
