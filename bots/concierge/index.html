Background

Why it exists

Once the crew grew past a dozen bots, Hank wanted someone whose only job was making the whole thing work better. He created the Concierge in early September to review the crew twice a week. It reports to the Chief of Staff, has no team of its own, and in October also became the crew's skeptic: the second opinion on any big decision.

Reports to: Chief of Staff

Purpose

Its mission

Make life easier by continuously improving how the bot crew serves you: a few high-leverage tweaks each review, a cross-check on risky decisions, and nothing changed without your OK.

Top skills

What it's good at.

  • Twice-weekly crew review: what's working, stuck, noisy or missing
  • Short optimization briefs with a few high-leverage fixes, not a wishlist
  • Skeptic review of big decisions: sign off, send back, or no
  • Checks the math and the facts before a decision reaches you
  • Price watch that pings you with a simple yes or not-now choice
  • Logs every verdict so there's a paper trail
How it's helped

Real work, from the crew's logs.

Each of these really happened in the owner's crew. Personal details are left out.

Sent back a travel quote with bad math

Asked to sign off on a flight option, it found the totals didn't add up and sent it back; after the travel bot revised it, the Concierge signed off and suggested a second option to show the owner.

Reviewed a list of new connectors

It worked through a proposed set of new connectors one by one, signing off on some and sending others back until there was a clear data-retention write-up or a concrete job.

Spotted a gap in the crew's rules

Its Monday check noticed that one routine wasn't named in the crew's permissions file; the Chief of Staff fixed the file the same morning.

Flagged an exposed secret

While reviewing a new access token, it noticed the secret had shown up in a work log and asked for it to be rotated and the old one confirmed dead.

Its team

Who reports to it.

Released bots glow and link to their stories. Gold is the next drop. Dimmed bots are coming, one a week.

No direct reports yet. The Concierge handles its lane on its own and reports straight to the Chief of Staff.
