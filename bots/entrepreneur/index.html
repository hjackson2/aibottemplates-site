Background

Why it exists

Hank wanted a bot whose whole job was spotting and vetting business ideas, so he added the Entrepreneur in early September as one of the first direct reports to the Chief of Staff. It owns the venture side of the crew, and the ventures it approves get run by their own specialist bots underneath it, from a recipe video channel to a small digital shop.

Reports to: Chief of Staff

Purpose

Its mission

Find and vet business ideas and side ventures, run the approved ones through a small crew of helper bots, and keep score honestly. It never spends, publishes, goes live or signs anything without your OK.

Top skills

What it's good at.

  • Weekly opportunity hunt against your hunt brief (sectors, time, risk)
  • Weekly scorecard that reports the real numbers, including zero
  • Delegates day-to-day venture work to its specialist bots
  • Checks public channel and shop stats before claiming progress
  • Sends short blurbs to your morning briefing bot after each run
  • Flags when an idea needs a professional instead of guessing
How it's helped

Real work, from the crew's logs.

Each of these really happened in the owner's crew. Personal details are left out.

Kept an honest weekly scorecard

Its weekly side-project scorecard pulled public channel stats and shop and email checks, and reported plainly when there were no sales yet, instead of dressing the numbers up.

Said what it couldn't check

When the owner's home computer connection was down, the scorecard listed exactly which dashboards it had not opened, rather than assuming they were fine.

Posted a launch, only after a go-ahead

It published a planned post for one of its ventures on X after its preflight checks passed, then confirmed the linked video was live.

New on this team

From one crew's side hustle to yours.

Hank's crew started with nothing but an idea and a few spare hours a week. Under the Entrepreneur, a small team of bots launched a cooking Shorts channel and a small digital shop from scratch. The bots did the legwork: they researched what people were searching for, drafted scripts, posts and listings, and pulled the real numbers together every week, including the weeks with nothing to show yet. Hank stayed in charge the whole time. Every post, every listing and every dollar spent waited for his OK.

Those specialist bots stay private, because they're built around Hank's own lanes. Side Hustle Builder is the general version of what they learned. Bring your own idea, and it interviews you about your time and budget, scores the idea, maps the risks, writes a 30-day plan and builds the team around it. You approve every move.
